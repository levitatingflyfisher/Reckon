The killer feature is not any single technique. It is your own track record confronting you.

Reckon is a decision journal built on a superforecasting "inner-crowd" protocol. The model never decides — it argues with itself so you can see the shape of the question. Your record is what earns weight.

• Log a decision, your confidence, and your reasoning
• An inner-crowd duel with distinct personas
• A deference map earned from your own past accuracy
• Group mode: everyone commits before anyone reveals
• Bring your own key, or run a model entirely on the device

Cloud models are strictly opt-in and bring-your-own-key. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you configure it and turn it on.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
